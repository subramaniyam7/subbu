How I work

Design process

01

Empathize

Conducting user interviews, surveys, and persona creation to deeply understand user needs, emotions, and pain points.

02

Define

Formulating problem statements, POV framing, and user journey maps to synthesize research and pinpoint core issues.

03

Ideate

Brainstorming, sketching, and crazy eights to generate a wide range of creative, out-of-the-box design concepts.

04

Prototype

Building interactive, high-fidelity clickable mockups in Figma and low-fidelity paper wireframes to bring ideas to life.

05

Test

Conducting usability testing, heuristic evaluation, and feedback sessions to validate and iteratively refine the solution.
